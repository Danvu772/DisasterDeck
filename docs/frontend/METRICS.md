# Desk metrics: research to system

Source: a research brief Dan brought in on 15 Nov (one standard or paper per bucket, each the origin of its metric). Rule from Dan: the metrics live in the chat. Ask the desk something, the answer comes with a chart that animates in, and keeps animating as the board changes. No headline numbers on the board (decision 2 in `DECISIONS.md`).

Contract: `components/metrics/types.ts`. Engine: t32. Charts: t33. Chat: t34. Animation rules: `research/emergency-map-animation.md` (motion only on a real state change, short, reduced motion respected).

| # | Metric (`MetricId`) | Source | Formula on our data | Data today | Chart |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `sla_t0` | NFPA 1710 percentile benchmarks ([DC FEMS, Evaluating Response Times](https://fems.dc.gov/page/evaluating-response-times)) | For tier-0 high-risk tasks: time from entering `waiting_approval` to the approval entry in `history`. Report the 90th percentile and the share within 5 min. Never the average | Task `history` with timestamps. Few approvals until the Brain dispatches (#8) | bars: p50, p90, share within 5 min |
| 2 | `freshness` | WIISARD data quality in mass-casualty response ([eScholarship](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4b66m6c4)) | quality = 0.5 ^ (age / halfLife). halfLife per record type = `STALE_AFTER_MIN` (quality 0.5 when a fact turns stale). Age from `observedAt` on the scenario clock (t31) | Every record has `observedAt` | bars: mean quality per record type, lowest records named |
| 3 | `credibility` | Admiralty code, NATO 6 x 6 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiralty_code)) | Two axes, never merged. Reliability A to F from the source (official person A, vetted person B, NASA and sheet B, news C, social posts D, unknown F). Credibility 1 to 6 from the fact (confirmed 1, corroborated 2, two or more reporters 3, one report 4, retracted 5, no data 6) | `sources`, `support`, `confidence`, user trust | matrix: 6 x 6 counts |
| 4 | `shelters` | FEMA ESF#6 Shelter System, formerly NSS ([FEMA NSS map service](https://gis.fema.gov/arcgis/rest/services/NSS/OpenShelters/MapServer)) | ESF#6 fields per shelter: name, status (open, full, closed), capacity, population, beds free, percent full, last update and its age | `Shelter` records | bars: used of total per shelter |
| 5 | `logistics` | Beamon & Balcik 2008, performance measurement in humanitarian relief chains ([Emerald](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/09513550810846087/full/html)) | Fill rate = quantity delivered / quantity requested. Unknown until then on live data | gauge: covered sites / open sites |
| 7 | `ics209` | FEMA ICS Form 209, Incident Status Summary ([FEMA](https://training.fema.gov/emiweb/is/icsresource/assets/ics%20forms/ics%20form%20209,%20incident%20status%20summary%20(v3).pdf)) | Snapshot: open incidents by severity, sites by status, people trapped, tasks by status, shelter population, roads closed, and the oldest fact in each line | Whole board | snapshot table |
| 8 | `lifelines` | FEMA Community Lifelines, Transportation ([DHS fact sheet](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/community-lifelines-fact-sheet)) | Road status counts (open, closed, unconfirmed), each with its evidence: sources and confirmation state. | 9 | `agent_fidelity` | FActScore, EMNLP 2023 ([arXiv 2305.14251](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251)) | Share of an agent reply's atomic facts that the board supports | Needs scenario ground truth and fact extraction. Unknown for the demo (Role 4 issue) | none |
| 10 | `triage_order` | Kendall's tau rank correlation (no single paper, stats 101) | tau between the desk's order (tier, then priority) and the true severity order | Needs the scenario's true severity. Unknown for the demo (Role 4 issue) | none |

Honesty rule: a metric without the data to back it returns `status: "unknown"` and `unknownReason`, and the chat says so in plain words. No made-up numbers, no placeholders that look real.
