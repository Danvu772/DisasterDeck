# Federal disaster-tech funding: DHS and FEMA

Nov 17, 2026 · Dan Vu

The NSF track (I-Corps → PESOSE → STTR) is in the [Desk](desk.md#funding-track).

The one door open right now is DHS S&T's Long Range BAA. It's standing until May 31, 2029, takes proposals from academia as well as companies, and starts with a conversation, not a proposal. DHS SBIR is the next one to watch. FEMA's grants go to states, not vendors, so that money is reached through a pilot partner.

## Signals worth knowing

- **DHS S&T is funding work on next-generation emergency operations centers.** It's working with PNNL on concepts, requirements and a vision for the next generation of EOCs, including better coordination for state and local emergency managers ([HSToday](https://www.hstoday.us/federal-pages/dhs/dhs-st-awards-funds-to-pnnl-to-research-the-future-of-emergency-management/)). That's DisasterDeck's lane, and PNNL is a contact worth having.
- **NUSTL's SAVER program** runs market research on commercial first-responder technology. Once DisasterDeck is a real product, answering one of their requests for information puts it on DHS's radar for free.
